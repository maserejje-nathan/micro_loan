# Changelog

All notable changes to LendFlow are documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-06-06

### Added

- Multi-tenant lending workspaces with role-based team access
- Customer management with KYC fields and document uploads
- Loan products, applications, approval workflow, and collateral
- Loan disbursement (cash, bank, mobile money) and repayment schedules
- Borrower self-service portal with online applications
- Platform super-admin area for organizations, billing, and settings
- Subscription plans with trials and usage limits
- SMS notifications (log driver + Africa's Talking)
- Mobile money integrations (stub + Yo! Payments)
- Audit logs, PDF loan statements, and operational reports
- Demo seeder with sample organization, customers, loans, and portal access
- Buyer documentation in `documentation/index.html`
